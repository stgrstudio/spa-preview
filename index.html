Before you book

Can't find your question? Ask us on WhatsApp. We usually reply within an hour, 8am–8pm.

What do I need to prepare at home?

About 2×2 metres of floor space, and a power outlet nearby for facials. We bring the table, mat, linens, towels and products.

I'm a complete beginner. Can I book training?

Yes. The first session starts with a mobility check, and the plan is built from what you can do comfortably today.

I have sensitive skin. Is a facial safe?

Tell us in the booking chat. We patch test on your first visit, skip anything harsh, and only do extractions if you want them.

Which products do you use?

BPOM-registered skincare, chosen for your skin type. If there are brands or ingredients you avoid, tell us before the visit.

How do deposits and rescheduling work?

A 30% deposit confirms your slot. Reschedule for free up to 24 hours before. Later changes keep the deposit as credit for your next visit.

Which areas do you cover?

Canggu, Seminyak and Ubud, with a fixed transport fee per area. For other areas, message us and we'll check the schedule.
